Frequently Asked Questions

How does the 28-day rule work? expand_more
The 28-day rule requires that your required maintenance funds must have been in your bank account for at least 28 consecutive days before the date of your application. The end date of this 28-day period must be within 31 days of the application date.
Accepted SOF sources expand_more
Valid sources of funds (SOF) include personal savings, proceeds from property sales, verified investments, and documented gifts from direct family members. All sources require comprehensive paper trails.
FX Buffer explanation expand_more
An FX (Foreign Exchange) buffer is an additional margin (typically 5-10%) added to your required funds to protect against currency fluctuations during the processing period, ensuring you don't fall below the minimum threshold.
